Node.js Classes for HTTP Servers
================================

net.Server
==========

Events
------

• close() - not emitted until all connections are ended.

• connection(socket: net.Socket) - new connection is made.

• error(err: Error) - ! note about "close" event.

• listening() - after calling "server.listen()".

• drop(data) - note "Server.maxConnections".

Methods
-------

• close(cb) - it's an async funcion.

• listen()
